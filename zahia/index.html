hello, zahia

أهلاً يا زاهية

picked for you, and getting better every time you tap a heart

keepsakes

everything you've tucked away

journal

only you can read this — it lives on your phone

earlier pages

your vibes

what i've learned you love so far

tap to tell me more

look

start over

clears what i've learned (your keepsakes and journal stay).
